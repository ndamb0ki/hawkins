Art Classes

Creating safe, expressive, and inspiring spaces for young artists.

Why These Art Classes Exist

Ndambuki Art Lab believes art is a language children already speak — sometimes louder with color than words. These sessions nurture confidence, creativity, and emotional expression in a community-centered environment.

What Children Learn

  • 🎨 Drawing, painting & mixed media
  • 🧠 Creative thinking & self-expression
  • 🤝 Teamwork through murals & group projects
  • 🌍 Cultural storytelling through art

How Sessions Are Run

  • Short creative warm-up
  • Main guided activity
  • Free expression time
  • Reflection & sharing

Who Can Join

Sessions are open to children of mixed age groups. Materials are adapted depending on age, ability, and context. Programs can run in schools, community centers, churches, or outdoor spaces.

Interested in Joining or Supporting?

Parents, collaborators, and sponsors are welcome. Reach out to discuss sessions, partnerships, or community programs.
